## 1. Tong Quan Yeu Cau

### Muc tieu kinh doanh
- Phat trien chuoi **50-100 cua hang ban le xe dap**
- Quan ly tap trung toan bo chuoi cua hang
- Tich hop ban hang da kenh (omnichannel)

### Cac tinh nang bat buoc
- **Quan ly ton kho**: Theo doi theo serial number/so khung xe, ma tran san pham (mau sac, kich co, dong xe, nam san xuat)
- **Quan ly nhieu cua hang**: Dong bo ton kho real-time, chuyen hang giua cua hang, bao cao theo tung chi nhanh
- **Quan ly khach hang (CRM)**: Lich su mua hang, bao hanh, cham soc khach hang, chuong trinh khach hang than thiet
- **Bao cao & phan tich**: Doanh thu, loi nhuan, hang ton, hieu suat nhan vien
- **Tich hop thuong mai dien tu**: Website, Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop
- **Quan ly nhan vien**: Cham cong, KPI, phan quyen
- **Quan ly chuoi cung ung**: Dat hang nha cung cap, theo doi don hang nhap
- **Dich vu sua chua & bao hanh**: Theo doi lenh sua chua, bao hanh khung xe, phu tung

## 3. Phan Mem Ban Hang Tong Hop Cap Doanh Nghiep

### 3.1 Shopify POS (voi Shopify Plus)

> Ho tro toi 1,000 cua hang ban le. Case study: Pepper Palace mo rong tu 40 den 100+ cua hang trong 5 nam.

**Tinh nang noi bat:**
- Thong nhat POS + e-commerce tren 1 nen tang
- Quan ly don hang tap trung
- BOPIS (Mua Online, Nhan Tai Cua Hang)
- Nhan vien khong gioi han voi theo doi hieu suat
- 100+ ung dung POS va tien ich mo rong
- API mo cho phat trien ung dung tuy chinh

**Bang gia (Shopify Plus):**

| Thanh phan | Chi phi |
|-----------|---------|
| Shopify Plus co ban | Tuy chinh (~$2,000+/thang) |
| POS Pro/dia diem | $89/thang (co the duoc mien khi dung Shopify Payments) |
| 20 dia diem POS Pro dau | Bao gom trong Plus |

**Uu diem:**
- E-commerce tot nhat voi POS thong nhat
- Da chung minh o quy mo 100+ cua hang
- He sinh thai ung dung khong lo
- Mien phi POS Pro o quy mo lon voi Shopify Payments
- API manh cho tich hop

**Nhuoc diem:**
- Khong chuyen cho xe dap (khong co quan ly sua chua)
- Can ung dung ben thu 3 cho quy trinh xe dap
- Gia Shopify Plus khong minh bach

## 4. Giai Phap Thi Truong Viet Nam

### 4.1 KiotViet - DAN DAU THI TRUONG VIET NAM

> 300,000+ cua hang su dung. Duoc KKR dau tu $45 trieu.

**Tinh nang noi bat:**
- Quan ly ton kho theo serial number/so khung xe (quan trong cho xe dap - bao hanh theo khung)
- Theo doi ton kho real-time voi canh bao het hang
- Tich hop may quet barcode va may in hoa don
- **Hoat dong offline** (tiep tuc ban khi mat mang)
- Tich hop san thuong mai dien tu: Tiki, Lazada, Shopee
- Tich hop doi tac van chuyen voi tu dong cap nhat trang thai
- Ho tro 20+ nganh ban le
- Bao cao doanh thu va loi nhuan real-time

**Bang gia:**

| Goi | Chi phi/thang |
|-----|--------------|
| Ho tro | 160,000 VND (~$6.50) |
| Chuyen nghiep | 240,000 VND (~$9.70) |
| Chi nhanh them | Tinh phi rieng |

*Mien phi cai dat, trien khai, nang cap va ho tro.*

**Quan ly chuoi (50+ cua hang):**
- Ho tro quan ly nhieu chi nhanh voi bao cao tap trung
- Mot so doanh nghiep tich hop KiotViet + Odoo ERP cho van hanh phuc tap hon
- Vi du: Be Bu Bam van hanh 50+ cua hang voi KiotViet + Odoo

**Uu diem:**
- Gia re nhat (chi phi thap nhat trong tat ca giai phap)

**Nhuoc diem:**
- Kha nang omnichannel han che hon Sapo

- Tuy chinh han che cho nhu cau phuc tap
- Voi 50+ cua hang, co the can them ERP (tich hop Odoo)

## 5. So Sanh Cloud vs On-Premise

| Tieu chi | Cloud | On-Premise | Hybrid |
|----------|-------|-----------|--------|
| **Chi phi ban dau** | Thap (thue bao) | Cao (phan cung + ban quyen) | Trung binh |
| **Chi phi hang thang** | Thue bao lien tuc | Thap (bao tri) | Trung binh |
| **Yeu cau internet** | Bat buoc | Khong | Mot phan |
| **Kha nang mo rong** | Xuat sac (them dia diem ngay) | Han che (can phan cung) | Tot |
| **Kiem soat du lieu** | Nha cung cap quan ly | Doanh nghiep kiem soat | Ket hop |
| **Tuy chinh** | Han che | Rong rai | Tot |
| **Cap nhat** | Tu dong | Thu cong (can IT) | Ket hop |
| **Dong bo nhieu cua hang** | Real-time, tu dong | Can ha tang rieng | Tot |
| **Hoat dong offline** | Han che/khong | Day du | Day du |
| **Can doi ngu IT** | Toi thieu | Can doi ngu rieng | Mot phan |

**Khuyen nghi:** Cloud hoac Hybrid. Cloud duoc khuyen nghi manh cho chuoi 50-100 cua hang nho dong bo ton kho real-time, quan ly tap trung, mo rong de dang. Hybrid (cloud + du phong local) la ly tuong cho Viet Nam khi internet chua on dinh o mot so dia diem.

> **Xu huong thi truong:** 61% nha ban le moi chon cloud POS thay vi on-premise truyen thong.

## 8. De Xuat Chien Luoc

### Phuong An A: Tap Trung Thi Truong Viet Nam (KHUYEN NGHI)

**De xuat: Sapo Enterprise + bo sung cong cu chuyen xe dap**

- Sapo Enterprise lam nen tang quan ly chuoi, omnichannel, tuan thu thue/van chuyen VN
- Bo sung phan mem quan ly sua chua/bao hanh rieng neu can
- Hieu qua chi phi, ho tro dia phuong, da chung minh quy mo lon tai VN
- **Phuong an thay the:** KiotViet + Odoo ERP (da chung minh voi 50+ cua hang - Be Bu Bam)

**Ly do chon:**
1. Tuan thu phap luat Viet Nam (hoa don dien tu, thue)
2. Tich hop san thuong mai dien tu noi dia (Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada)
3. Tich hop van chuyen noi dia (GHN, GHTK, Viettel Post...)
4. Ho tro tieng Viet, doi ngu ho tro tai VN
5. Chi phi hop ly cho quy mo 50-100 cua hang

### Phuong An B: Uu Tien Tinh Nang Chuyen Xe Dap

**De xuat: Lightspeed Retail (goi Core hoac Plus)**

- POS chuyen xe dap da chung minh nhat o quy mo nhieu cua hang
- Quan ly ton kho tot nhat cho xe dap
- Omnichannel va e-commerce manh
- Can tich hop them cho dac thu thi truong VN

### Phuong An C: Uu Tien E-commerce Ket Hop Cua Hang

**De xuat: Shopify Plus**

- Nen tang thuong mai truc tuyen + offline thong nhat tot nhat
- Da chung minh o quy mo 100+ cua hang
- Mien phi POS Pro khi dung Shopify Payments
- Bo sung app chuyen xe dap tu Shopify App Store

### Phuong An D: Doanh Nghiep Lon Voi Chuoi Cung Ung Phuc Tap

**De xuat: Oracle NetSuite + POS chuyen xe dap**

- Khi chuoi kiem ca phan phoi, san xuat hoac chuoi cung ung phuc tap
- NetSuite cho back-office ERP + Lightspeed/Ascend cho POS tai cua hang
- Chi phi cao nhat nhung toan dien nhat

### Phuong An E: Ket Hop (KHUYEN NGHI CAO CHO CHUOI XE DAP VN)

**De xuat: Sapo Enterprise (hoac KiotViet Enterprise) + module Lightspeed/Workstand**

- Nen tang VN lam xuong song cho tuan thu, van chuyen, thanh toan noi dia
- Module quoc te chuyen xe dap cho quan ly ton kho va sua chua
- Tich hop API giua cac he thong
- Can bang nhu cau thi truong noi dia voi tinh nang chuyen biet nganh xe dap

## 9. Ke Hoach Trien Khai

### Giai doan 1: Pilot (5-10 cua hang)
- Lua chon va trien khai phan mem tai 5-10 cua hang pilot
- Ap dung bat ke chon nen tang nao
- Danh gia hieu suat, phan hoi nhan vien, loi phat sinh

### Giai doan 2: Di chuyen du lieu
- Lap ke hoach 2-4 thang cho di chuyen du lieu tu he thong cu
- Lam sach va chuan hoa du lieu san pham (SKU, danh muc, serial number)
- Chuan hoa quy trinh truoc khi mo rong

### Giai doan 3: Ha tang internet
- Dam bao moi cua hang co internet on dinh voi ket noi du phong
- Xem xet he thong hybrid/offline (KiotViet, Ascend) neu ket noi khong on dinh

### Giai doan 4: Dao tao
- Dao tao toan dien cho nhan vien
- Lightspeed cung cap huong dan 1-1 mien phi
- Nen tang VN ho tro tieng Viet

### Giai doan 5: Tich hop
- Ket noi POS voi ke toan (MISA, SAP)
- Tich hop e-commerce (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop)
- Tich hop doi tac van chuyen

### Giai doan 6: Mo rong 50-100 cua hang
- Trien khai lan luot theo cum 10-20 cua hang
- Theo doi va toi uu sau moi dot mo rong

## Ket Luan

Doi voi chuoi ban le xe dap 50-100 cua hang tai Viet Nam, **Phuong An A (Sapo Enterprise)** hoac **Phuong An E (Ket Hop)** la phu hop nhat vi:

1. **Tuan thu dia phuong**: Hoa don dien tu, thue, van chuyen VN
2. **Chi phi hop ly**: Thap hon dang ke so voi giai phap quoc te
3. **Ho tro noi dia**: Doi ngu ho tro tieng Viet, am hieu thi truong
